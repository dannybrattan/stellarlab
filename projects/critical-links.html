Critical phenomena and quantum field theory

A curated set of accessible background pages, reviews and lecture notes related to this research programme.

Wikipedia primers

Accessible starting points for the ideas that recur throughout this programme.

Critical phenomenaScaling, universality, critical exponents and diverging correlation lengths near continuous phase transitions.Conformal field theoryQuantum and statistical field theories with conformal symmetry, central to the description of many critical points.Renormalisation groupHow physical descriptions change with scale, and why fixed points organise universal critical behaviour.Conformal bootstrapA non-perturbative programme that constrains conformal field theories using symmetry, unitarity and crossing consistency.Topological quantum field theoryField theories whose observables capture topological information and which describe many topologically ordered phases.Monte Carlo methods in statistical physicsNumerical sampling methods used to study statistical systems and compare lattice calculations with continuum predictions.
